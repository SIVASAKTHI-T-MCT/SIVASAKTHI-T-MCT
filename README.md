## 🚀 Featured Engineering Projects

My projects cover multiple stages of engineering development, from initial concepts and CAD models to hardware prototypes, software integration, and simulation. The implementation and validation level varies by project.

### 🔬 Research Project

**PEM Fuel Cell Monitoring & Intelligent Energy System**

Exploring a proton exchange membrane fuel cell system for hydrogen-to-electricity conversion, with a proposed integration of PLC-based control, SCADA monitoring, and machine learning.

**Technical areas:** Fuel cell systems, embedded monitoring, industrial automation, and intelligent control.

### 🏆 Competition Projects

**1. MotorMind AI — Motor Health Monitoring System**

An embedded monitoring and machine learning project exploring motor condition assessment using sensor data and microcontroller-based hardware.

- Sensor-based motor parameter monitoring
- Data acquisition and condition classification
- Exploration of machine learning for fault detection
- Investigation of remaining useful life (RUL) estimation

**Technical areas:** Embedded systems, motor control, machine learning, and predictive maintenance.

**2. Hydraulic Pump Health Monitoring System**

A condition-monitoring concept focused on observing hydraulic pump operating behaviour and exploring methods to identify abnormal conditions through sensor data.

**Technical areas:** Sensors, data acquisition, fault detection, and predictive maintenance.

**3. Eddy Current-Based Crack Detection System**

A non-destructive testing concept exploring eddy-current sensing for detecting potential cracks or material discontinuities.

**Technical areas:** Electromagnetic sensing, defect detection, signal analysis, and industrial inspection.

**4. Mine Digital Twin**

A simulation-oriented project exploring a virtual mine environment connected to operational telemetry for monitoring and what-if analysis.

- Virtual open-pit mine environment
- Telemetry-driven simulation concepts
- UAV/UGV-assisted data collection architecture
- Exploration of Unity-based digital twin workflows

**Technical areas:** Digital twins, simulation, robotics, and industrial monitoring.

### 🎓 Academic Projects

**5. Crowd Monitoring System**

A sensor-based system using an ultrasonic sensor and servo motor to explore crowd or occupancy monitoring and automated actuation.

**Technical areas:** Arduino, ultrasonic sensing, servo control, and embedded systems.

**6. Self-Healing DC–DC Converter Using Machine Learning**

An exploratory project combining power conversion and machine learning to investigate fault identification and potential recovery strategies.

**Technical areas:** Power electronics, intelligent monitoring, machine learning, and control systems.

### 🏭 Company-Oriented Projects

**7. SCARA-Based Saree Printing Robot**

A robotic mechanism concept designed for automated fabric printing or stamping, with an emphasis on mechanical design and motion.

- CAD modelling of robot links and mechanical assemblies
- Joint and mechanism arrangement
- Motor-driven motion concepts
- Printing-tool end-effector design

**Technical areas:** Robotics, CAD, mechanism design, and motion control.

**8. Vision-Based Defect Sorting System**

A machine-vision automation concept exploring image-based defect identification and the sorting of inspected objects.

**Technical areas:** Computer vision, machine learning, automated inspection, and electromechanical integration.

### 🤖 Robotics & Autonomous Systems

**9. ROS 2 Drone and Rover System**

A robotics project exploring a drone–rover workflow in simulation, with the drone intended for visual observation and the rover intended for ground-level sensing and navigation.

**Technical areas:** ROS 2, Gazebo, robot simulation, computer vision, and autonomous systems.
